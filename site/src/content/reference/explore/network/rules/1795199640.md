# Block employee clients from pfSense management ports.

**Firewall rule ID:** `1795199640` · **Action:** **BLOCK** · **Interface:** [CLIENT](/reference/explore/network/zones/CLIENT/), position **1**

## What does it do?

This security rule was added after the CR-015 review found unwanted workstation reachability to pfSense management. It is first on CLIENT, ahead of the general allow.

## Operational detail and recovery note

The hypervisor name is **nova-client01**, but the verified current Windows/AD computer identity is **NOVA-CLIENT**. They identify the same guest at different layers. Do not silently rename one to the other.

A running virtual machine does not automatically mean its services are healthy. Explore the linked service tests to see how it performs.
